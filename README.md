## Labs and Practice Tickets

### ServiceDesk Simulator

I use simulated support scenarios to practice a repeatable support workflow:

1. Review the ticket and identify the reported issue.
2. Gather relevant details and identify the likely scope.
3. Troubleshoot methodically and record the steps taken.
4. Apply the resolution available in the simulation.
5. Verify the result and update the ticket notes.
6. Identify next steps or escalation options if the issue remains unresolved.

Planned or in-progress practice examples may include:

- Password reset and account-access scenarios
- Account lockout troubleshooting
- Basic network connectivity issues
- Software and workstation support
- Ticket documentation and escalation

## How I Document Each Lab

Each lab or ticket aims to include:

- **Objective:** What I am trying to learn
- **Scenario:** The reported issue or task
- **Environment:** The simulator, learning platform, or test environment used
- **Troubleshooting:** The steps I actually performed
- **Resolution:** What action addressed the issue, if resolved
- **Verification:** How I checked the result
- **Lessons learned:** What I learned and what I would investigate next
- **Evidence:** Relevant screenshots or outputs, with sensitive information removed
